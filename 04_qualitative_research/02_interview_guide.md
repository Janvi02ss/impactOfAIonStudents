# Semi-Structured Interview Guide

## Participant target
8–12 students with variation in AI use, year and field.

## Opening
Explain that the goal is to understand experiences, not to judge AI use. Ask permission before recording. Avoid collecting unnecessary personal information.

## Questions
1. Tell me about the last time you used AI for studying.
2. What exactly did you ask it to do?
3. What did you do with the answer?
4. Did it save time? What happened to that saved time?
5. Tell me about a time you did not trust an AI answer.
6. Do you verify AI output? How?
7. Have you noticed any change in your writing, problem solving, memory, creativity or confidence?
8. When does AI feel like a tutor? When does it feel like a shortcut?
9. Have you ever used AI when you knew you could do the task yourself?
10. How do your peers influence your AI use?
11. Are your institution's rules clear?
12. What support do you want from teachers or your institution?
13. What do you think responsible AI use looks like?
14. What would you tell a first-year student about using AI?

## Follow-up prompts
- “Can you give me an example?”
- “What happened next?”
- “How did that make you feel about your own ability?”
- “What made you decide to trust/check/reject the answer?”
