# Primary Student Survey — Ready-to-Use Instrument

## Intro
This anonymous survey explores how college/university students use generative AI for studying and how they perceive its effects on learning, productivity, thinking and academic work. Do not collect names or contact information unless separately required for an interview and consented to.
