# Qualitative Coding Framework

## Step 1 — Open coding
Tag meaningful phrases without forcing them into a pre-decided conclusion.

## Step 2 — Suggested codebook
**Benefits:** time saving, organisation, explanation, feedback, confidence, accessibility, productivity.

**Learning:** understanding, practice, memory, skill development, critical thinking, creativity.

**Substitution:** direct answers, copy/adapt, outsourced thinking, reduced effort.

**Trust:** verification, hallucination, source checking, confidence, uncertainty.

**Integrity:** cheating, unclear rules, citation, fairness, peer pressure.

**Emotion:** anxiety, relief, frustration, confidence, fear of falling behind.

**Institution:** policy clarity, teacher guidance, AI training, assessment changes.

**Future:** employability, AI literacy, career expectations.

## Step 3 — Theme construction
Cluster codes into themes:
1. AI as accelerator
2. AI as substitute
3. Trust and verification
4. Skill development and erosion
5. Institutional support gap
6. Unequal access/capability
7. Future readiness

## Validation
Keep negative and positive cases. Do not select quotes only because they support the desired story.
