# AI & Its Impact on Students — Assignment 1 Research Pack

## Working understanding
**Topic:** Impact of generative AI on students, with emphasis on the tension between **AI as a learning accelerator** and **AI as a learning substitute**.

The project should NOT start by assuming AI is simply good or bad. The research should test:
- what students use AI for;
- how intensity and type of use relate to study behaviour;
- what students perceive as benefits;
- where students report dependency, skill erosion, anxiety, or integrity concerns;
- how institutional guidance changes the context;
- whether different student profiles experience AI differently.

## Important dataset note
The supplied 50,000-row CSV is included as a working quantitative dataset. Its internal quality checks are clean, but **the file does not contain provenance/source metadata**. Until the original source is documented, treat it as a supplied/synthetic/working dataset rather than claiming that it represents a real-world population.

## Package map
- `01_research_plan/` — phase-wise plan, research questions, sampling, methodology and rubric mapping.
- `02_data/` — raw supplied CSV, cleaned/prepared CSV and data dictionary.
- `03_external_evidence/` — traceable external study-level evidence matrix.
- `04_qualitative_research/` — survey/interview instruments and coding framework.
- `05_analysis/` — exploratory summaries and correlation matrix from the supplied dataset.
- `06_ai_documentation/` — prompt log and AI-validation protocol.
- `07_future_design_handoff/` — a deliberately brief future-facing Next.js/data-story handoff; research remains the priority.

## Recommended evidence hierarchy
1. Primary data you collect yourself.
2. Published survey datasets / supporting spreadsheets from reputable research organisations.
3. Peer-reviewed research.
4. Institutional reports.
5. News coverage only for context, not as the primary evidence when the original report is available.

## Core caution
Correlation in the supplied dataset does not establish that AI caused a change in GPA, retention, anxiety, or burnout. Use wording such as **“associated with”**, **“students reporting higher…”**, or **“in this dataset…”** unless a source provides causal evidence.
