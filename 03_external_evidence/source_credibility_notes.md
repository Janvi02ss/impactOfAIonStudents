# Source Credibility Notes

## Tier 1 — Strong starting sources
- HEPI 2026 Student Generative AI Survey: large, recent undergraduate survey with downloadable supporting data.
- Jisc 2025 Student perceptions of AI: mixed qualitative/survey evidence and detailed student discussion themes.
- EDUCAUSE 2025 Students and Technology Survey: useful higher-education survey instrument and structured questions.
- Peer-reviewed studies in journals such as *The Internet and Higher Education*, *Computers & Education Open*, *Education and Information Technologies*.

## Tier 2 — Context
- UNESCO guidance for education/research: strong institutional context, but it is guidance rather than a student-outcome dataset.
- EY-Parthenon/FICCI: useful India HEI policy/adoption context, but only 30 institutions.

## Tier 3 — Secondary reporting
Use news articles to discover studies, then cite the original study/report whenever possible.

## Provenance rule
Every number in the final presentation should be traceable to:
- a dataset row/calculation;
- a published report/table;
- a peer-reviewed paper;
- or your own primary survey/interview data.
